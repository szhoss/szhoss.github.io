I have always been interested in many things: books, science, philosophy, history, technology, photography, and the world around me. I enjoy reading, exploring unfamiliar ideas, and occasionally making things with my own hands.

Yet I have often found that there is a gap between consuming ideas and making something of them. We read countless pages, encounter an endless stream of information, and have thoughts that come and go. But how much of it do we really take the time to understand, reflect upon, and put into our own words?

Perhaps this is one of the reasons I decided to start this blog.

## Reading, Thinking, Making

The subtitle of this blog is *Reading · Thinking · Making*. These three words describe, in a rather simple way, what I hope to explore here.

**Reading** is where many of my journeys begin. Books allow me to encounter minds, experiences, and worlds far beyond my own. I don't believe in reading merely to accumulate knowledge or complete a list of books. I would rather follow my curiosity, read slowly, and let one book lead me to another.

**Thinking** is what happens when ideas meet experience. Sometimes a passage in a book raises a question; sometimes an ordinary encounter makes me reconsider something I have long taken for granted. I want to give these thoughts room to develop, even when they are incomplete or uncertain.

**Making** is an attempt to turn curiosity into something tangible. It might be a piece of software, a photographic experiment, a small craft project, or simply an idea brought into reality. I have come to believe that tools are valuable not for their own sake, but for what they enable us to create.

These three activities are not separate pursuits. Reading inspires thinking; thinking leads to making; and making, in turn, gives us new reasons to read and think.

## A Place for Ideas to Grow

I don't intend to turn this blog into a comprehensive knowledge base, nor do I want to impose a rigid publishing schedule upon myself.

I have spent quite some time experimenting with note-taking systems and ways of organizing knowledge. Eventually, I realized that I was sometimes spending more time building the tools than actually using them. I would rather write something imperfect today than spend another month designing the perfect system for writing.

This blog is meant to be a place where I can put things into words: observations, questions, discoveries, reflections, and perhaps a few mistakes. Not every thought needs to become a polished essay. Not every question needs an immediate answer.

I also don't expect my opinions to remain unchanged. Some things I write here may eventually be revised, reconsidered, or contradicted by something I learn later. I see no problem with that. Changing one's mind is part of understanding the world.

## An Open Beginning

I don't know exactly what this blog will become, and I don't think I need to know just yet.

Perhaps, over time, it will become a collection of essays, reading notes, experiments, and fragments of thought. Perhaps some subjects will occupy me for years, while others will disappear after a brief encounter.

Whatever happens, I hope this little corner of the internet will become a record of curiosity: a place where I can follow questions without always knowing where they will lead.

For now, that is enough.
